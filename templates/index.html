Key Metrics Explained:
  • Precision: What percentage of tweets flagged as a category actually belonged to that category. (Minimizes false positives).
  • Recall: What percentage of actual tweets in a category were successfully caught by the model. (Minimizes false negatives).
  • F1-Score: The harmonic mean of precision and recall. Provides a balanced metric for highly imbalanced datasets.
